## Method

The current MatMix production image is the working reference. For each MAT, the public card title was compared with brand/model/variant/colour/weight text visible on the pack and package form. Pixel fill is an automated estimate of the largest foreground bounding-box dimension divided by the limiting canvas side (foreground threshold: max RGB deviation from white >10).

REFRAME changes only whitespace crop, proportional scale, centering and white canvas. It does not redraw or retouch the pack, logo, label, text or colours. Prepared source files are 1200×1200 PNG. Previews were generated by the unchanged importer renderWebp() pipeline: contain 1080×1080, white background, extend 60 px each side, WebP quality 82. Changed source fill is 95.3–95.4%; importer-preview fill is 85.3–85.9%.
